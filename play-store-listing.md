# Play Store listing — Brightpath

## App name
Brightpath: Accessible Learning

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Learning that adapts to the learner — for students, teachers, and parents.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Brightpath is a learning support platform built for students with dyslexia — and everyone
around them who wants to help.

One lesson, understood in whatever way makes it click. Teachers upload a worksheet or lesson
once, and Brightpath adapts it into accessible reading, audio, simplified explanations,
vocabulary support, and grounded practice questions — all reviewed and approved by the teacher
before a student ever sees it.

FOR STUDENTS
• A distraction-free reader with adjustable font, spacing, and background
• Real text-to-speech with paragraph, sentence, or word highlighting
• Select any text to ask an AI tutor for an explanation or a word's meaning
• Practice questions generated only from material you've actually read
• Track your own progress — practice completed, not a score

FOR TEACHERS
• Upload once, preview every adaptation before publishing
• See which students may benefit from extra support — without labels
• Create classes, assignments, and track submissions in one place

FOR PARENTS
• See what your child is working on and how it's going
• Get short, practical suggestions for supporting learning at home
• Message your child's teacher directly

FOR SCHOOLS
• A school-wide view of classes, teachers, and accessibility usage
• Approve teacher and parent access before any data is shared

Every account's data is protected at the database level — a parent can only ever see their own
linked child, a teacher only their own students. AI never makes final decisions about a
student's work; a human always reviews before anything is published.

Brightpath does not diagnose dyslexia or any other condition. It's a tool for making existing
material more accessible, not a clinical or medical product.
